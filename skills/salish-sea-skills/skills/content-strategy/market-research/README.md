# Market Research: ICP Language Mapping

**What:** Scrape Reddit + web for ICP complaints, frustrations, and exact language.  
**When:** You're building messaging and copy but don't know how your customers actually talk.  
**Output:** ICP language map + copy angles + messaging framework.

## Use Cases

- You're repositioning and need fresh messaging (not what *you* think; what they say)
- You're building landing pages and need realistic objections/fears
- You're training sales team — what's the actual language in their market?
- You're stuck in consultant-speak and need to hear real customer problems

## The Skill

This skill runs a tiered research pipeline:

1. **Haiku scraping:** Find Reddit threads, forum posts, reviews where your ICP discusses problems
2. **Sonnet analysis:** Extract frustrations, objections, exact quotes
3. **Opus synthesis:** Patterns, messaging angles, language map

Output: The actual words your customers use (not your interpretation).

## Prerequisites

- Clear ICP definition (who are you building for? What problem?)
- Willingness to read raw customer language (it won't be polished)

## Getting Started

```
Use the market-research methodology to understand how [your ICP] talks about [your problem]
```

Claude will:
1. Search Reddit, forums, reviews for raw ICP conversations
2. Extract frustrations and exact language
3. Build a language map: what words do they use? What do they fear?
4. Suggest copy angles based on real language (not marketing-speak)

## Time Investment

- **First run:** 4–5 hours (includes deep research)
- **Iterations:** 2–3 hours (refreshing for new audience segment, new positioning)
