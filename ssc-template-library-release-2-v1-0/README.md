# Salish Sea Consulting Template Library — Release 2

This release contains the next six priority products approved from the SSC Rebrand template backlog. The assets are designed for editing in Microsoft Word or Excel and compatible office suites.

| Product | File | Primary workflow |
|---|---|---|
| Grant Report | `ssc-grant-report-template-v1-0.docx` | Compare approved commitments with actual outputs, outcomes, evidence, financial performance, variance, learning, and next steps. |
| RFP Response | `ssc-rfp-response-template-v1-0.docx` | Capture requirements, organize a compliant evaluator-oriented response, align evidence, work plan, staffing, risk, and price, and perform final submission QA. |
| Grant Letter of Intent | `ssc-grant-letter-of-intent-template-v1-0.docx` | Confirm funder fit and draft a concise inquiry with need, approach, results, credibility, request, and next step. |
| Grant Tracking Spreadsheet | `ssc-grant-tracking-spreadsheet-v1-0.xlsx` | Manage prospects, applications, requirements, deadlines, awards, reporting obligations, contacts, and portfolio metrics. |
| Nonprofit Board Meeting Minutes | `ssc-nonprofit-board-meeting-minutes-template-v1-0.docx` | Record notice, attendance, quorum, decisions, motions, votes, recusals, executive session, actions, approval, and retention. |
| Volunteer Agreement | `ssc-volunteer-agreement-template-v1-0.docx` | Define volunteer role expectations and identify situations requiring separate policies, forms, or qualified legal review. |

## Recommended use

Begin with the instruction page or the workbook’s Instructions sheet. Replace bracketed prompts, delete example rows after confirming their purpose, and retain an untouched master copy. Follow the current funder, issuer, board, organizational, and jurisdictional requirements that govern the real work.

The Volunteer Agreement is a working draft for attorney review. It intentionally does not supply automatic waiver, indemnity, intellectual-property assignment, medical authorization, media-release, background-check consent, or arbitration boilerplate. The Board Meeting Minutes template likewise requires review against applicable law, governing documents, and adopted board rules.

## Quality assurance

All six files passed automated integrity and structure checks. The Grant Tracking workbook passed formula, validation, table, chart, metadata, and sheet-architecture checks. All six products were rendered and visually reviewed for clipping, accidental blank pages, table continuity, readable hierarchy, and balanced pagination.
