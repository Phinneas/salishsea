In practical terms, it's the skill of mentally stepping into your customer's world and seeing things through their eyes.</p><p>When you master theory of mind in copywriting, you stop writing about what <em>you</em> think is important about your product and start addressing what your <em>customer</em> genuinely cares about. This shift transforms generic copy into messaging that feels personally crafted for each reader.</p><p>When applying Theory of Mind to copywriting, remember that your brand is not the hero—your customer is. While you might be tempted to tell your brand's story, effective storytelling copywriting keeps the focus on understanding your customer's journey, challenges, and desires.
