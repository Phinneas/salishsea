**California's first mandatory emissions reports are due November 10, 2026.** That's the nearest hard deadline on this list. Everything else is either pending, on hold or already in force.

This page tracks the status of each major ESG and climate disclosure rule. Status means where the rule stands legally today: in force, pending, on hold or proposed.

> **What this tracks:** ESG, climate disclosure and green-claims rules that affect US companies and EU-active companies.
> **How to read it:** each row gives who it covers, where it stands and the next date to watch.
> **Method:** each row is checked against a primary source or at least one law-firm or regulator summary. Sources are linked below the table.
> **Last updated:** November 2, 2026. Rows change. Check the date before you rely on one.

## Status at a Glance

| Rule | Who it covers | Status | Next date |
|---|---|---|---|
| **California SB 253** (emissions reporting) | Companies above $1B revenue doing business in California | **In force.** CARB's initial regulation took effect October 12, 2026. | **Nov 10, 2026:** first Scope 1 and 2 reports due |
| **California SB 261** (climate-risk reports) | Companies above $500M revenue doing business in California | **On hold.** Ninth Circuit injunction pending appeal since November 18, 2025. | Ninth Circuit ruling (no date set) |
| **SEC climate disclosure rule** | US public companies | **Never took effect.** Rescission proposed May 29, 2026. | Final rescission vote (no date set) |
| **EU CSRD** (after Omnibus I) | EU companies with 1,000+ employees and €450M+ turnover. Non-EU groups with €450M+ EU turnover. | **Amended.** Omnibus in force March 18, 2026. | Mar 19, 2027: member-state transposition deadline |
| **CSRD value-chain cap** | Suppliers with up to 1,000 employees | **In force** with the Omnibus. Suppliers can decline requests beyond the voluntary standard. | Ongoing |
| **Revised ESRS** | Companies in CSRD scope | **Adopted** by the Commission July 3, 2026. Over 60% fewer mandatory datapoints. | Nov 20, 2026: expected entry into force. FY2027: mandatory. |
| **EU EmpCo Directive** (green claims) | Anyone marketing to EU consumers | **In force** since September 27, 2026. Bans generic claims and offset-based product neutrality claims. | National enforcement. Several member states haven't transposed. |
| **FTC Green Guides** | Environmental marketing claims in the US | **In force** (2012 version). Review open since 2022. No revision issued. | No date set |
| **Washington Climate Commitment Act** | Emitters above 25,000 tCO2e, fuel suppliers, utilities | **In force.** Linkage with California and Québec signed June 25, 2026. | 2027: target for joint market |
| **Oregon Climate Protection Program** | Gas utilities, fuel suppliers, large industrial sources | **In force** since January 2025. New legal challenge filed April 16, 2026. | Oregon Court of Appeals ruling (no date set) |
| **Washington SB 6092** (corporate emissions reporting) | Proposed California-style Scope 1 to 3 reporting | **Not enacted.** Passed the Senate in 2024 but never became law. | None |

## Notes by Rule

### California SB 253 and SB 261

CARB's initial regulation was approved and took effect October 12, 2026. It confirmed November 10, 2026 as the first SB 253 deadline, moved from August 10. [Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/insights/memo/2026/October/CARB-Initial-Regulation-Confirms-November-10-Reporting-Deadline)

The Ninth Circuit enjoined SB 261 on November 18, 2025, while the Chamber of Commerce appeal continues. It declined to enjoin SB 253. [Sullivan & Cromwell](https://www.sullcrom.com/insights/memo/2026/October/CARB-Initial-Regulation-Confirms-November-10-Reporting-Deadline), [King & Spalding](https://www.kslaw.com/Insights/Articles/California-Extends-SB-253-Greenhouse-Gas-Reporting-Deadline-and-Signals-Clarifying-Rule-Changes)

### SEC climate rule

The SEC proposed rescinding its 2024 climate rule in full on May 29, 2026. The Eighth Circuit case stays in abeyance while the rescission proceeds. [Harvard EELP](https://eelp.law.harvard.edu/tracker/financial-regulation-climate-change-and-climate-related-risk-disclosure/), [DLA Piper](https://marketedge.dlapiper.com/2026/06/sec-published-a-proposal-to-formally-rescind-its-landmark-2024-climate-related-disclosure-rules/)

### CSRD, the value-chain cap and the revised ESRS

The Omnibus I Directive was published February 26, 2026 and took effect March 18, 2026. It raised the thresholds to 1,000 employees and €450 million in turnover. [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/EU-Sustainability-Omnibus-Published-in-the-Official-Journal) Large companies now report from financial year 2027. Companies with up to 1,000 employees are protected by a value-chain cap. [Accountancy Europe](https://accountancyeurope.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/260129-Omnibus-explained-CSRD-factsheet.pdf)

The Commission adopted the revised ESRS on July 3, 2026. Mandatory datapoints fall by more than 60%. They're expected to enter into force November 20, 2026 and apply from January 1, 2027, with optional use for financial year 2026. [Rödl & Partner](https://www.roedl.com/en/insights/sustainability-reporting-publication-of-the-revised-esrs-as-a-delegated-act/), [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-07-21-european-commission-adopts-revised-eu-csrd-reporting-standards)

For what each framework is for, see [ESG Frameworks Explained](/blog/esg-frameworks-explained).

### EU EmpCo (Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition)

EmpCo has applied since September 27, 2026, with no transition period. It bans generic claims like "green" or "eco" without recognized excellent performance. It also bans uncertified sustainability labels and "climate neutral" product claims based on offsets. Future claims like net-zero pledges need a detailed plan verified by an independent expert. Eight member states hadn't notified national measures as of late October 2026. [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/fr/insights/publications/2026/10/the-eu-directive-on-empowering-consumers-for-the-green-transition-two-years-on-what-it-requires-and-where-the-member-states-stand)

### FTC Green Guides

The Green Guides were last updated in 2012. The FTC opened a review in 2022, but no revision has been issued. [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/whats-next-for-the-green-guides-insights-from-commissioner-holyoaks-remarks-at-cpg) They're still the reference point for US green-claims cases. See the [Greenwashing Enforcement Tracker](/blog/greenwashing-enforcement-tracker/).

### Washington and Oregon

For the full regional picture, see [ESG and Sustainability Communications in Washington and Oregon](/blog/esg-sustainability-communications-washington-oregon/).

- **CCA:** Washington, California and Québec signed a linkage agreement June 25, 2026. Washington's linkage rules took effect October 24, 2026. [Washington Department of Ecology](https://ecology.wa.gov/air-climate/climate-commitment-act/cap-and-invest/linkage)
- **Oregon CPP:** Nearly 30 petitioners led by Oregon Business & Industry asked the Court of Appeals to rule the program exceeds the commission's authority. [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/04/16/oregon-climate-protection-program-lawsuit/)
- **SB 6092:** Would have required Scope 1 to 3 reporting with third-party verification. It passed the Senate in 2024 and wasn't enacted. [ALL4](https://www.all4inc.com/4-the-record-articles/washington-sb-6092-greenhouse-gas-rule/)
