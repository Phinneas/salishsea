# Content Plan: "Reasoning Lift" — Full Series

**Source:** Kevin Indig, Growth Memo (May 18, 2026) — *Reasoning lift: What happens to AI visibility when AI thinks harder*
**Research date:** May 2026
**Audience:** SEO/growth marketers, content strategists, brand managers across B2B SaaS, Finance, Health/Lifestyle, Consumer Tech

---

## Core Thesis

> AI reasoning mode is a second, parallel search engine — and most brands are only optimizing for one.

---

## The Data Foundation

### Source type shifts, minimal → high reasoning

| Source type | Minimal | High | Direction |
|-------------|---------|------|-----------|
| Brands | 62.4% | 60.6% | Slight drop |
| Official docs | 12.4% | 17.5% | Gains |
| UGC / review sites | 14.3% | 6% | Halved |
| Reddit | 15% | 7% | Halved |
| Gov / academic | 1.9% | 8.8% | Quadruples |
| Media | 8.9% | 7.1% | Slight drop |

### Citation rate lift by vertical

| Vertical | Low reasoning | High reasoning | Delta |
|----------|--------------|----------------|-------|
| Finance | 52% | 80% | +28pp |
| Health/Lifestyle | 48% | 72% | +24pp |
| B2B SaaS | 40% | 56% | +16pp |
| Consumer Tech | 60% | 64% | +4pp |

### Other key stats

- Citation rate overall: 50% (minimal) → 68% (high) — +18pp
- Average sources per response: 2.6 (minimal) → 4.5 (high)
- Fan-out queries: 4.6x more under high reasoning (245 vs. 1,130 across 100 prompts)
- Unique domains cited: 127 (minimal) vs. 173 (high) — 99 domains appear only under high reasoning
- Domain overlap between modes: only 25.6% shared
- Brand persistence Problem→Selection: 0/20 journeys (minimal) vs. 4/20 journeys (high)
- Fan-out at Comparison stage: 5.5 (minimal) vs. 24 (high)
- Fan-out at Selection stage: 2.6 (minimal) vs. 15.4 (high)
