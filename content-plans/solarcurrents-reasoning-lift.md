# Content Plan: Reasoning Lift Applied to Solar Currents
**Source research:** Kevin Indig, Growth Memo — *Reasoning Lift* (May 18, 2026)
**Site:** solarcurrents.co — honest solar guides, real-world derates, portable solar, RV/van life, home/renter solar
**Audience:** Campers, RV/van lifers, renters, and homeowners researching solar — skeptical, practical, not ideological

---

## Core Thesis

> Solar Currents' "honest math, real-world derates" positioning is structurally identical to what high-reasoning AI treats as an authoritative reference source — but the content needs Problem-stage entry points and regulatory depth to earn citations before buyers know what they're searching for.

---

## Vertical Reality Check

Solar Currents sits at a useful intersection. Surface-level, it's Consumer Tech (+4pp) — the worst vertical for reasoning lift. But the site covers three content areas that trigger higher-reasoning behavior:

| Content area | Closest vertical | Why it qualifies |
|---|---|---|
| Solar generators / portable power | Consumer Tech (+4pp) | Product reviews, roundups — low lift on their own |
| Home backup / grid-tied systems | Finance (+28pp) | Utility interconnection, NEC codes, permits, net metering regulations |
| Renters / balcony solar | Health/Lifestyle (+24pp) | Legal compliance, lease terms, safety certifications, state/country-specific rules |

The site's "skepticism-first" voice — explicit derating, honest efficiency math, real output vs. marketing specs — mirrors exactly what authoritative reference sources do. That positioning is the right instinct. The gap is structural: most existing content is Exploration/Comparison stage, and the regulatory depth that earns Finance/Health-level citations is thin.

## Pillar 1 — The Honest Math Positioning (Biggest Structural Advantage)

Solar Currents already does what high-reasoning AI prefers: explicit efficiency factors, real-world output calculations, named derating percentages. The problem is this positioning is stated in individual posts but never structured as a standalone reference a reader (or AI) can cite as a primary source.

**What high reasoning looks for in a solar generator query:**
- Rated vs. actual output (what derating factors apply?)
- Panel wattage under real conditions (not STC)
- Battery usable capacity vs. rated capacity (LiFePO4 80–90% DoD vs. AGM 50%)
- Recharge time: solar only vs. AC vs. car
- Surge wattage for motor loads (CPAP, refrigerators, power tools)
- Pass-through charging: can it charge and discharge simultaneously?
- UL/ETL certification and what it covers

| Format | Title | Core angle |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Evergreen guide | "How to Read a Solar Generator Spec Sheet (What Marketing Math Hides)" | Structures the honest-math positioning as a reference document. Every spec buyers encounter — rated vs. actual, surge vs. continuous, solar input ceiling — with the math to derate it correctly. Citeable as a primary reference under high reasoning. |
| Blog | "The Real-World Solar Generator Output Calculator" | Interactive or step-by-step guide for calculating actual output under the reader's conditions (location, season, panel angle). Gov/academic source territory when built with explicit NREL/PVWatts methodology. |
| Structured data page | "Solar Generator Spec Database: Real-World Derates for Major Brands" | One reference page with structured spec tables for Jackery, EcoFlow, Bluetti, Anker, Goal Zero — covering all 7 sub-query dimensions. Citable like a product documentation page. |

## Pillar 2 — Problem-Stage Gaps (The Persistence Opportunity)

Every existing post on Solar Currents is Exploration or Comparison stage. Under high reasoning, Problem-stage citations persist to Selection in 4/20 journeys. Solar Currents has zero Problem-stage entry points — meaning it never enters the journey before buyers know they want a solar product.

**Three buyer journeys, all missing Stage 1:**

*Camping/outdoor journey:*
1. **Problem:** *"My devices die on every trip" / "I can't run my CPAP camping"*
2. **Exploration:** *"What's the best portable solar setup for camping?"*
3. **Comparison:** *"Solar generator vs power bank vs panel + battery"*
4. **Validation:** *"Will this actually run [specific device]?"*
5. **Selection:** *"Best solar generator under $500 for camping"*

*RV/van life journey:*
1. **Problem:** *"Shore power costs are eating my travel budget" / "I can't dry camp without running out of power"*
2. **Exploration:** *"How much solar does an RV actually need?"*
3. **Comparison:** *"Solar kit vs DIY install vs generator"*
4. **Validation:** *"Will this setup run my AC / microwave / slide?"*
5. **Selection:** *"Best RV solar kit for full-time living"*

*Renter/home journey:*
1. **Problem:** *"I want solar but I rent and can't install panels"*
2. **Exploration:** *"Can renters use solar?"*
3. **Comparison:** *"Balcony solar vs solar generator for apartments"*
4. **Validation:** *"Is balcony solar legal in my state / country?"*
5. **Selection:** *"Best plug-in solar panel for apartment balcony"*

|--------|-------|------------|
| Blog | "Why You Keep Running Out of Power at Camp (It's Not Your Battery)" | Stage 1 (Problem) for camping/outdoor. The real causes: undersized panels, poor panel placement, partial shading, DOD confusion. Diagnostic framing makes this a reference document, not a roundup. |
| Blog | "5 Signs Your RV Power Setup Isn't Built for the Way You Camp" | Stage 1 (Problem) for RV/van life. Symptoms that indicate solar undersizing or battery mismatch — before the reader knows they need a new setup. |

## Pillar 3 — Sub-Query Spec Optimization (Comparison Stage)

When high reasoning gets "best solar generator for RV," it fires 24 sub-queries. Solar Currents' comparison content answers the parent query (which product wins) but not the sub-queries that high reasoning uses to verify the recommendation.

**What sub-queries fire on a solar generator comparison:**
- Maximum solar input wattage (what size panel array can it accept?)
- AC charging speed (hours to full from wall)
- Simultaneous solar + AC charging?
- LFP vs. NMC battery chemistry (cycle life, safety, cold weather)
- MPPT vs. PWM charge controller (efficiency difference)
- Pass-through efficiency loss
- Expandable battery? (which models accept add-on packs)
- Weight per Wh stored
- Warranty: years, cycle count, pro-rated or replacement

| Format | Title | Core angle |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Content upgrade | Retrofit existing comparisons with structured spec tables | Add sub-query sections to the top comparison posts. One spec per named section, explicit numbers, no narrative padding. This is the highest-ROI edit on the site. |
| Deep-dive series | "The Complete [Brand] Solar Generator Spec Sheet" (Jackery / EcoFlow / Bluetti / Anker) | One page per major brand covering all 9 sub-query dimensions. Structured for AI retrieval the same way a manufacturer datasheet is — but honest about real-world derates. |
| FAQ series | "Real Answers to Solar Generator Questions" | One URL per common question, direct answer in first paragraph. Replaces the Reddit thread function with structured, citeable content. |

## Pillar 4 — Regulatory & Utility Content (The Finance/Health Angle)

Gov/academic sources quadruple under high reasoning (1.9%→8.8%). For solar, those sources are **NEC 2020/2023 electrical codes, NREL PVWatts data, utility interconnection agreements, state net metering regulations, and UL/ETL safety certifications**. Solar Currents has no content in this category — and the honest-math positioning makes it the natural editorial layer for this material.

**What sub-queries fire on "home solar backup" or "renters solar":**
- Is a permit required for a solar generator? (battery storage)
- NEC Article 706 for battery storage systems
- Utility interconnection requirements (grid-tied vs. off-grid)
- Net metering rules by state
- Balcony solar regulations by US state and EU country
- UL 9540A and what battery fire testing means
- HOA solar restrictions

| Format | Title | Core angle |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Evergreen guide | "Do You Need a Permit for a Solar Generator or Home Battery? (State-by-State)" | High-stakes compliance question with state-level variation. Structures NEC and local code requirements accessibly. Makes Solar Currents citeable alongside .gov and .edu sources. |
| Evergreen guide | "Balcony Solar Regulations in the US: What's Legal, What's Gray, What's Banned" | 500,000+ German households demonstrate demand. The US regulatory picture is fragmented and genuinely confusing. First authoritative guide on this wins the citation. |
| Evergreen guide | "Net Metering Explained: What Your Utility Owes You (And What It Doesn't)" | Regulatory complexity triggers Finance-level authoritative retrieval. State-by-state variation, recent rollbacks (California NEM 3.0), what to check before going grid-tied. |

## Pillar 5 — The Renters Market (The Differentiation Play)

The renters/balcony solar content is Solar Currents' most differentiated territory — and the most underserved segment in solar content. The "Best Solar Setups Renters Can Install Without Landlord Drama" post is the right instinct. The gap is depth: one post covering an entire market segment can't win sub-queries the way a content cluster can.

**What sub-queries fire on "solar for renters":**
- Plug-in solar panel power output (real vs. rated)
- Which states allow balcony solar?
- HOA vs. landlord restrictions — who has authority?
- Can you take a balcony solar setup when you move?
- Best window-mounted solar panels for apartments
- Micro-inverter vs. battery for balcony solar
- What size setup can an apartment balcony realistically support?

|--------|-------|------------|
