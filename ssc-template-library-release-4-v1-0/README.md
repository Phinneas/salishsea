# Salish Sea Consulting Template Library — Release 4

This release contains ten additional products: nonprofit governance and finance documents, plus operational finance workbooks. Files are designed for editing in Microsoft Word or Excel and compatible office suites.

| Product | File | Primary workflow |
|---|---|---|
| Donation Acknowledgment Letter | `ssc-donation-acknowledgment-letter-template-v1-0.docx` | Issue compliant, donor-ready acknowledgment letters that document gifts for tax purposes. |
| Fiscal Sponsorship Agreement | `ssc-fiscal-sponsorship-agreement-template-v1-0.docx` | Draft a fiscal sponsorship agreement defining the sponsor relationship, responsibilities, and fees. |
| LLC Operating Agreement | `ssc-llc-operating-agreement-template-v1-0.docx` | Draft an LLC operating agreement covering ownership, management, contributions, distributions, and dissolution. |
| Nonprofit Bylaws | `ssc-nonprofit-bylaws-template-v1-0.docx` | Draft nonprofit bylaws covering governance, board, officers, meetings, amendments, and dissolution. |
| Conflict of Interest Policy | `ssc-nonprofit-conflict-of-interest-policy-template-v1-0.docx` | Draft a nonprofit conflict-of-interest policy with disclosure, recusal, and review procedures. |
| Memorandum of Understanding | `ssc-nonprofit-memorandum-of-understanding-template-v1-0.docx` | Draft an MOU defining a partnership's shared intent, roles, and expectations. |
| Budget vs. Actual | `ssc-budget-vs-actual-template-v1-0.xlsx` | Track budget against actuals by month and category, with variance analysis. |
| Cash Flow Forecast | `ssc-cash-flow-forecast-template-v1-0.xlsx` | Project cash inflows and outflows to spot shortfalls before they happen. |
| KPI Dashboard | `ssc-kpi-dashboard-template-v1-0.xlsx` | Track key performance indicators with targets and status in a single dashboard. |
| Profit & Loss Statement | `ssc-profit-and-loss-statement-template-v1-0.xlsx` | Build a monthly profit-and-loss statement with revenue, costs, and net income. |

## Recommended use

Replace bracketed prompts, preserve an untouched master copy, and follow the current legal, accounting, funder, and organizational requirements governing the real work. The agreement and governance documents (Fiscal Sponsorship, LLC Operating Agreement, Bylaws, Conflict of Interest Policy, Memorandum of Understanding) are working drafts for qualified attorney review, not complete agreements.
